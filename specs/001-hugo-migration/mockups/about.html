About This Blog

I started this blog in 2012 as a place to document what I was learning about DevOps, continuous delivery, and monitoring. Over the years it's expanded to cover infrastructure automation, team leadership, and the cultural aspects of building effective engineering organizations.

The posts here are a mix of technical tutorials, conference talk summaries, and reflections on what it means to lead technology teams. Most of the technical content focuses on the monitoring and observability space — an area I've been deeply involved in throughout my career.

Professional Background

I'm an engineering leader based in Madison, Wisconsin. My work spans infrastructure, developer experience, and building teams that ship reliable software. I've spoken at DevOpsDays conferences about web performance monitoring, innovation in creative companies, and the partnership between technology and creative teams.
